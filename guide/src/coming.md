# Coming next

Later chapters, following the rest of the Verus guide:

- **Multi-turn invariants**: model a battle as state transitions and prove a property holds on every turn, not just in one decision.
- **Opponents**: reason about what another robot can do to you.
- **"Eventually attacks"**: liveness, or proving something good eventually happens.
- **Quantifiers over sequences**: properties of whole scan histories with `forall` and `exists`.
- **Integer geometry for bearings**: prove that a computed bearing really points at the target, instead of trusting the runner's floating-point trigonometry.
